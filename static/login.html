अनिवार्य सत्यापन प्रक्रियाएं / Standard Operating Procedures

  • 🚗
    HSRP Number Plate Optical Verification (CMVR 1989 Rule 115A)

    Sub-second optical plate recognition compatible with standard state plates and BH-series Bharat registration numbers prior to nozzle unlock.

  • 🧪
    PESO 3-Year Hydrostatic Stretch Testing (Rule 35)

    Zero-tolerance automated interlock: immediate dispensing lockout if cylinder hydro-test certificate exceeds 3-year statutory validity.

  • ⚡
    MODBUS-RTU Hardware Solenoid Interlock

    Sub-100ms hardware electrical relay cut-off switch wired directly into dispenser solenoid valves for automated emergency fuel stop.

  • 📋
    Cryptographic Electronic Safety Ledger (SHA-256)

    Immutable cryptographic audit records of all inspection decisions, timestamps, and operator dispensing actions.
